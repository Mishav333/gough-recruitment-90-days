Built into the new site: GoughAI

Think ChatGPT, built only for property, construction and development.

GoughAI runs on a leading AI model, chosen with you and set up to know your market.
Candidates and clients can ask it anything in your world: what a role pays, how to step up from site manager to project director, or what to look for when hiring one.
It answers like one of your specialists, drawing on your Salary Report where it applies. Then it leads each person to the right next step: a live job, the right consultant, or a brief for your team.
It stays in your lane. Your team sets what it will and won’t say, and anything serious goes to a person.

What Gough gets from it

  1. Your expertise, open around the clock.It answers at 10pm on a Sunday, when your consultants are off.
  2. Warmer enquiries.Every hand-over arrives with the full conversation, so your consultant starts the call already knowing what they need.
  3. A read on your market.See what candidates and clients ask about, week by week: roles, pay, locations.
  4. The go-to voice in your sector.People come to Gough to understand property careers, not just to scroll a job board.
